TRES REYES ISLANDS

Gasan, Marinduque

It is an island group that consists of three main islands with a crystal clear blue-green waters and white sand that makes it ideal for island hopping and snorkling. These islands are named after the Three Kings, namely Melchor Island, Gaspar Island, and Baltazar Island.

POCTOY WHITE BEACH

Torrijos, Marinduque

Why spend a lot on Boracay, when you can also enjoy a fine white sand beach in the heart of the Philippines for cheaper costs and but with the same scenic view.

LUZON DATUM OF 1911

Mogpog, Marinduque

Another scenic view where you can almost see the entire island of Marinduque. It is not just for aesthetics, it is also historical, The Luzon Datum of 1911 is the primary geodetic reference point for the Philippines, established at Station Balanacan in Marinduque to unify national surveys and mapping.
